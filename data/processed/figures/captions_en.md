# Experimental figure captions

**Figure 4.** Bifurcation diagram of the real-valued logistic map. The dashed line marks the period-doubling accumulation point. The shaded region marks the nominal control-parameter interval of the Q64 prototype.

**Figure 6.** Empirical Shannon entropy per output byte for the two fusion paths under simulated uniform inputs. Each of the five paired batches contains 20,000 eight-byte outputs per method. Lines connect the same batch across methods. Diamonds and error bars show the mean and sample standard deviation.

**Figure 7.** Low-lag autocorrelation of random sequences generated by the Original HQC and Enhanced HQC prototypes.

**Figure 8.** Bit change rate distributions after perturbing the seed (a), initial state (b), or control parameter (c) of the Q64 logistic branch.

**Figure 9.** Bit change rate distributions in key sensitivity tests of the Original HQC and Enhanced HQC prototypes.

**Figure 10.** Bit change rate distributions in plaintext avalanche tests of the Original HQC and Enhanced HQC prototypes.

**Figure 11.** Number of distinct outputs divided by the total number of outputs for the three configurations at primary-source support exponent \(h=8\). The primary-source support contains \(2^h\) possible values. Each configuration generated 3,000 outputs.

**Figure 12.** Empirical maximum-frequency score (a) and fraction of distinct outputs (b) as functions of primary-source support exponent \(h\). The score is the negative base-two logarithm of the largest observed output frequency. Each configuration generated 3,000 outputs per setting, giving a score ceiling of \(\log_2 3000\).

**Figure 13.** Fraction of trials in which the shared secret changed after bit flips in ciphertext component \(u\) (a), component \(v\) (b), or the full ciphertext (c).

**Figure 14.** Mean bit change rate of the shared secret after bit flips in ciphertext component \(u\) (a), component \(v\) (b), or the full ciphertext (c).
